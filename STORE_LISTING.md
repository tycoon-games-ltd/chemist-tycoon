## Full description
Open your first shutter in Kolkata and grow a pharmacy into a chain. Set prices, stock and staff, handle surprises, and decide when to expand.

Two editions in one app:
- Real Life: income tax, payroll costs, manager attention, e pharmacy competition, inspections, landlord hikes and bandhs. Slower, smaller wins.
- Classic: a more forgiving version with every feature.

What you can do
- Pick an area on a map of 18 Kolkata neighbourhoods, with rent, rival and profit forecasts.
- Run month by month: discounts, generics share, stock cover, helpers, marketing, delivery.
- Grow with 11 add ons: delivery app, doctor chamber, lab tests, care club, wellness corner, equipment rental, vaccination clinic, chronic care, home nursing, own brand generics and institutional supply.
- Weather real events: monsoon, dengue season, power cuts, lockdowns, recessions and rival moves.
- Set yearly goals, earn 50 plus badges, review each year, and compare results with a score code.
- Save slots, export and import, and play offline.

This is a game. Figures and events are simulated. It is not medical, legal or financial advice.
