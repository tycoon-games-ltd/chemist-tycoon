Chemist Tycoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Summary

Chemist Tycoon does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, saved slots, settings and best score are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. If you export a save code, it is text that you copy yourself. Deleting the app, or using Erase all saved data in Settings, removes it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • No third party services that receive your data.

Fiction and advice

The game is a simulation. Business figures, medical services, taxes, events and places are imagined or simplified for play. Nothing in the game is medical, legal, tax or financial advice.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.
